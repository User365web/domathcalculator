About Domathcalculator

Domathcalculator is a free collection of calculation tools for students, engineers and curious people. Our aim is simple: a clean, fast and accurate scientific calculator, a reliable unit converter and practical engineering formulas in one place, with no sign-up and no clutter.

Our principles

  • Accuracy first – formulas and conversion factors use standard published values (SI, NIST).
  • Privacy by design – calculations happen in your browser; we never receive your numbers.
  • Speed – lightweight pages that work well on slow connections and small screens.
  • Transparency – the site is supported by advertising so it can stay free. See our Privacy Policy.
