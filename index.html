An advanced calculator for students and engineers

Domathcalculator was built for people who need dependable answers quickly: engineering and physics students, technicians, designers and anyone who works with numbers. The calculator supports sine, cosine and tangent with their inverses and hyperbolic forms, base-10 and natural logarithms, square and cube roots, exponentials, percentages, factorials, the constants π and e, and the previous answer (Ans).

Expressions are evaluated with a safe parser that respects operator precedence, nested parentheses and implicit multiplication such as 2π or (1+2)(3+4). A live preview shows the result as you type, and every calculation is saved to a local history so you can reuse it.

Built for engineering work

Switch to the unit converter to move between metric and imperial units, or open the engineering tools for ready-made formulas covering electrical circuits, structures, fluids and thermodynamics. Read the guide to learn every function and shortcut.
